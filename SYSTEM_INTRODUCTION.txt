MINSU BUSINESS CENTER MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
System Introduction and Description

The MinSU Business Center Management System is a web-based platform designed to centralize the daily business operations of the Mindoro State University (MinSU) Business Center. It gives authorized administrators and staff a single system for managing products, stock, sales transactions, personnel, reports, business-center rental spaces, and toga rentals.

The system helps replace manual and scattered records with organized, searchable, and traceable digital information. Its dashboard provides an at-a-glance view of operational activity, while the individual modules support the full workflow from product setup and inventory monitoring through order processing and reporting.

CORE BUSINESS AND INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

The system allows users to create and organize product categories, maintain product details, and monitor available inventory. Stock can be restocked and inventory movements are recorded through product transactions. Product information can include important operational details such as pricing, status, expiration information, and product images.

For daily sales, the order-processing module supports the creation and management of orders, completion of transactions, sales-history review, and voiding of orders when necessary. A staff-facing point-of-sale interface is also included for processing transactions. Payment and invoice-related records are supported by the backend data model.

MANAGEMENT AND REPORTING

The dashboard, reporting, and sales-analytics features help administrators monitor business performance. These areas provide information needed for reviewing sales, inventory activity, product transactions, and operational summaries. Reports can be prepared and exported through the reporting module to support management review and decision-making.

The system also includes staff and account-management functions. Administrators can manage user accounts, user roles, staff information, profile settings, and password changes. Access is protected by authentication and is intended to support role-based use by administrators and staff.

RENTAL MANAGEMENT

The rental module manages business-center properties or spaces that are rented to outside tenants, such as canteen operators, boarding-house operators, photocopy services, or other business tenants. It keeps related rental records together, including:

- Rental properties and their location, rate, status, amenities, and stall number
- Tenant personal and business details
- Rental contracts, lease periods, deposits, payment terms, and renewal status
- Rental payments, due dates, payment status, and late fees
- Maintenance requests, repair priorities, assignments, costs, and completion status

This module enables the Business Center to monitor occupied and vacant spaces, active contracts, rental income, pending or overdue payments, and maintenance concerns in one place.

TOGA RENTAL MANAGEMENT

The system includes a separate toga-rental module for managing graduation toga rental operations. It supports the management of departments, student or rental records, rental payments, and module statistics. This allows the Business Center to organize toga rentals and payment tracking by academic department.

TECHNOLOGY OVERVIEW

The application uses a React-based frontend with Vite for the user interface and a Laravel backend for business logic and APIs. Data is stored in a relational database such as MySQL or SQLite. The backend uses Laravel authentication and API-token support, while the frontend uses a responsive web interface designed for desktop and mobile use.

OVERALL PURPOSE

In summary, the MinSU Business Center Management System is an integrated operations platform for handling Business Center products, stock, sales, staff accounts, reports, property rentals, and toga rentals. Its purpose is to improve record accuracy, make daily work faster, strengthen accountability, and give administrators clearer information for managing Business Center services and revenue.
